EDVYRO TASK 2 — REPRODUCIBLE VALIDATION RULES

Dataset: healthcare_patients_sample.csv
Purpose: privacy-aware governance and cleaning of synthetic/de-identified patient records.

Rules:
1. PatientID must be non-empty, match PAT-### format, and be unique.
2. Every field must be non-empty; missing values are logged rather than clinically imputed.
3. Age must be an integer in the validation range 0–120.
4. Gender must be one of the supplied categories: Male or Female.
5. DosageMg must be numeric and greater than 0.
6. TreatmentDurationDays must be a positive integer.
7. Readmitted must be Yes or No.
8. BloodPressure must contain numeric systolic/diastolic components, both greater than 0.
9. CholesterolLevel must be numeric and greater than 0.
10. No clinical value is invented, estimated, or replaced during cleaning.

Result for supplied 10-row sample:
- Missing values: 0
- Duplicate Patient IDs: 0
- Validation exceptions: 0
- Cleaned output is unchanged from the supplied records because no exception required a correction.

Note:
The supplied dataset does not define clinical reference ranges or measurement units for every clinical field. Therefore, this task applies structural/data-quality validation rather than claiming medical normality.
